Batiyan Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-08-05

Batiyan is a multilingual real-time chat app. This policy explains what data may be processed when you use the app.

Information We Process

  • Chat messages you choose to send.
  • Nickname/avatar selected in the app.
  • Selected language and app preferences stored locally on your device.
  • A browser visitor ID stored locally to count visits/users.
  • Basic technical data such as IP address may be processed by Cloudflare for security and delivery.
  • Reported messages may be stored for moderation review.

How We Use Data

  • To deliver chat messages in real time.
  • To translate messages into selected languages.
  • To maintain app safety, prevent spam, and review abuse reports.
  • To show usage statistics such as current online users and visit counts.

User-Generated Content

Messages are user-generated content. Do not share private, illegal, hateful, sexual, harassing, or harmful content. Messages may be visible to other users in the same chat room.

Third-Party Services

Batiyan may use Cloudflare services for hosting, security, storage, and real-time delivery. Translation may use external translation providers.

Data Removal / Contact

For removal requests, abuse reports, or privacy questions, contact: YOUR_SUPPORT_EMAIL@example.com

Changes

This policy may be updated as the app improves.
