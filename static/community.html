Batiyan Community Guidelines

Last updated: 2026-08-05

Batiyan is for respectful multilingual communication.

Be Respectful

  • Do not harass, threaten, or target people.
  • Do not post hate speech or violent content.
  • Do not post sexual or exploitative content.
  • Do not spam, scam, or impersonate others.
  • Use the Report button when you see abuse.

Moderation

Reported content may be reviewed and removed. Repeated abuse may lead to restrictions.
